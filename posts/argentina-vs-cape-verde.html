🔍 Tactical Review

Argentina began with the composed authority of a defending champion - Messi dropping into pockets between the lines, Almada and Mac Allister rotating wide to stretch Cabo Verde's compact 4-3-3 block. The breakthrough came from a different pattern entirely: Lisandro Martínez's perfectly weighted long ball over the top for Messi's sharp run, controlled immaculately and smashed into the roof of the net for his 20th career World Cup goal. It was the kind of moment that felt like it would settle the occasion. Instead, Cabo Verde refused to accept the script.

Cabo Verde's gameplan was executed with remarkable discipline. The Blue Sharks' press forced repeated Argentine turnovers in the middle third, and their equaliser was a product of genuine tactical belief - Ryan Mendes' surging run down the right, a low cross into the box, and Deroy Duarte's composed finish through the legs of both Lisandro Martínez and an unsighted Emiliano Martínez. From that moment, the debutants played without fear, absorbing Argentina's second-half pressure, protecting Vozinha superbly, and forcing extra time with growing belief that they could actually win.

The decisive tactical phase came in extra time, where Argentina's physical set-piece presence eventually told - Lisandro Martínez lashing home a fierce half-volley from a crowded corner melee, Cabo Verde responding instantly through Sidny Lopes Cabral's unbelievable curling strike into the top corner (instantly hailed as a goal-of-the-tournament contender), and finally the cruelest moment: Cristian Romero's powerful header from a Messi corner taking a wicked deflection off Diney Borges and rolling into the net in the 111th minute. Cabo Verde deserved to lose on the scoreboard but not on merit - a performance that announced their arrival on the global stage.

⚽ Goal: Messi Makes History (1-0, 29')

Following a mid-half hydration break, Argentina looked to break through Cabo Verde's low defensive block. Lisandro Martínez spotted Messi making a sharp, timed run from a central area behind the backline. Martínez lofted a perfectly weighted, pinpoint long ball from midfield. Messi controlled it masterfully with his left foot, drew out the 40-year-old goalkeeper Vozinha, and with a quick second touch from his left foot, smashed a high strike into the roof of the net from an acute angle. This marked his historic 20th career World Cup goal.

⚽ Goal: Duarte Stuns the Champions (1-1, 59')

Argentina dominated second-half possession, but a slick counter-attack down the right flank completely turned the game. Ryan Mendes aggressively carried the ball into the final third and whipped a low cross past Facundo Medina into a pocket of space inside the box. Midfielder Deroy Duarte made a late run, controlled the cross, and acted without hesitation. He fired a low, right-footed shot from a difficult angle that slipped cleanly through the legs of defender Lisandro Martínez and beat an un-sighted Emiliano Martínez.

⚽ Goal: L. Martínez Capitalizes on Chaos (2-1, 92')

After regulation ended 1-1 due to brilliant saves by Vozinha, Argentina won a corner kick just two minutes into extra time. Messi delivered a dangerous in-swinging corner that created an intense, crowded melee inside Cabo Verde's six-yard box. Alexis Mac Allister kept the play alive, helping flick the ball down. The ball bounced into the path of Lisandro Martínez, who alertly anticipated the rebound and lashed a fierce half-volley over Vozinha at the near post to reclaim the lead.

⚽ Goal: Lopes Cabral's World-Class Stunner (2-2, 103')

Refusing to back down, the Blue Sharks fought their way back into Argentina's half. Yannick Semedo distributed a sharp pass wide to the left flank, finding left-back Sidny Lopes Cabral driving forward into open space. Cabral cut inside on the edge of the 18-yard box and unleashed an unbelievable, curling right-footed strike that arced past the outstretched arms of Emiliano Martínez, kissing the inside of the far top-right corner. Instantly hailed as a contender for goal of the tournament.

⚽ Goal: The Heartbreaking Own Goal (3-2, 111')

With a penalty shootout looming, Argentina earned another crucial corner on the right side. Messi stepped up again, using his left foot to whip a high, curling ball toward the near-post area. Center-back Cristian Romero leaped above the crowd to connect with a powerful header. The ball took a massive, wicked deflection off Cabo Verde defender Diney Borges, completely altering its trajectory and leaving Vozinha stranded as it rolled into the back of the net. A cruel end for a side that had given everything.

Not vintage Messi across 120 minutes - the first half was his best, the second half saw his influence wane as Cabo Verde's midfield grew in confidence and squeezed his space. But that 29th-minute goal — a perfectly weighted long ball from Lisandro Martínez, controlled with his left foot and smashed into the roof of the net — was a moment of class that only he can produce. His 20th World Cup goal extends his all-time record and keeps Argentina's title defence alive. His corner deliveries in extra time also proved decisive, creating the chaos that led to both extra-time goals. This was a night when the team around him needed to carry him across the finish line, not the other way around, but his moments of quality were still match-defining.

💡 Key Takeaways & What's Next
  • Cabo Verde's tactical discipline was extraordinary — their press forced repeated Argentine turnovers, and their response to every setback (equalising twice, including a goal-of-the-tournament contender) showed genuine footballing quality, not just defensive resilience.
  • Argentina's set-piece prowess in extra time saved them — both extra-time goals came from Messi corners, highlighting the value of dead-ball situations when open-play creativity dries up against a disciplined low block.
  • Argentina's squad depth through extra time was ultimately the difference, but the defending champions need significant improvement to retain the title — Cabo Verde exposed real vulnerabilities in transition that better teams will ruthlessly exploit.

Argentina face Egypt in the Round of 16. Cabo Verde exit as heroes of the tournament, having won hearts across the football world.
